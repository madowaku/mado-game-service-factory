MADO PLAYTEST EVIDENCE - SUPPORT

Preview version: 0.4.0

For preview testing, report reproducible issues at:

https://github.com/madowaku/mado-game-service-factory/issues

Please include:
- product version
- Windows version
- command used
- Godot version, when relevant
- adapter/report status
- the smallest safe log excerpt that reproduces the problem

Do not attach confidential game source, screenshots, or unreleased assets unless
you intend to share them publicly in the issue.
